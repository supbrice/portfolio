About

IT professional in Minnesota focused on systems, support, cloud, networking, and building technology that works.

I’m Ngu Brice Che — an IT professional who enjoys solving technical problems, supporting people, and keeping systems reliable and productive.

My Background

My career has taken me through several sides of IT — from hands-on technical support at MTN Cameroon, to systems administration at nVent HOFFMAN, where I worked with Azure, identity, Terraform, and PowerShell automation.

I’ve also worked in workforce operations analysis at Willis Towers Watson (WTW), technical customer support at Foundever, and most recently client technical support at Roundel (Target).

Across these experiences, I’ve built a foundation in Tier 2/3 support, identity and access, hybrid infrastructure, troubleshooting, and SLA-focused operations.

What I Do

My core focus is:

Systems Administration · IT Operations · Technical Support · Networking

Alongside that foundation, I work with Azure, Entra ID / Active Directory, hybrid cloud, Terraform, PowerShell, and Python.

I hold Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104) and Azure Solutions Architect Expert (AZ-305) certifications. I’m also intentionally building toward cybersecurity and security operations, continuing to expand my technical skill set as I grow.

Beyond IT
